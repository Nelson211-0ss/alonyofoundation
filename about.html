Our Story

Alonyo Foundation grew out of a simple belief: the people closest to a problem are the ones best equipped to solve it. Here's how that belief became a movement.

Our Mission

To partner with communities in Magwi County, South Sudan to break the cycle of poverty through education, clean water, health care, and economic empowerment — building the resilience to turn hardship into lasting hope.

Our Vision

A future where every community has the resources, skills, and opportunity to thrive on its own terms — where support from outside is a bridge, never a crutch.

Why "Alonyo"

A name born from hardship, chosen on purpose.

In Acholi tradition, "Alonyo" is a name given to a child born into difficult circumstances — a quiet, defiant statement that hard beginnings don't determine what comes next. Our founders chose it deliberately: every community we work with is writing that same story, on its own terms.

It's also why our brand leans into green — the color of new growth pushing up through hard ground.

Our journey

From one village to a movement

2015

A grassroots beginning

A small group of volunteers in Omeo Payam began helping families resettle and rebuild after decades of regional conflict — starting with school fees and emergency food support for 40 households.

2017

First borehole, first scholarship class

Alonyo Foundation formally registered as a community-based organization, drilled its first borehole in Omeo, and enrolled its first cohort of 25 scholarship students.

2019

Women's savings groups launch

Five pilot savings-and-loan groups formed in Nimule — the seed of what is now a network of over 300 groups and 6,200 members.

2021

Expansion into health & agriculture

Mobile health clinics and climate-smart farming training launched in response to community-identified gaps, reaching Pageri and Obbo payams.

2023

Cross-border partnerships

Programs extended to additional payams across Magwi County, with cross-border health referral partnerships supporting Acholi communities just over the Uganda border.

2026

120,000+ lives impacted

Today, Alonyo Foundation works across 45 communities with a team of 38 staff — over 90% of whom are from the payams we serve.

What guides us

Our core values

Community-Led

Local leaders set priorities; we bring resources and technical support to plans they already own.

Dignity First

No poverty photography, no savior narratives — every story we tell centers the person living it.

Radical Transparency

Every dollar tracked, every report public. Ask us anything about where funds go — we'll show you.

Sustainable Impact

We measure success by what keeps working after we've moved on to the next village.

Partnership, Not Charity

We fund and support local organizations rather than parachuting in as the sole solution.

Rooted Growth

Real change is slow and local. We invest for the decade, not the news cycle.
