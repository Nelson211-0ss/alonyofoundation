Our Programs

Six interconnected areas of work, all designed to reinforce each other — because a child can't stay in school without clean water, and a farm can't grow without a healthy family working it.

Education Clean Water Health Women's Empowerment Agriculture Resilience
Program 01

Education & Futures

We cover school fees, supplies, and exam costs for students who would otherwise drop out — then back that up with mentorship, teacher training, and school infrastructure so the whole system improves, not just one child's odds.

  • ✅ Secondary & vocational scholarships, prioritizing girls and orphaned children
  • ✅ Mentorship circles pairing students with local professionals
  • ✅ Teacher training workshops in literacy and numeracy instruction
  • ✅ Classroom and latrine construction at partner primary schools
3,400+ students supported · 94% secondary completion rate among scholars
Program 02

Clean Water & Sanitation

Water fetching can eat up to five hours a day — hours that come out of school, farming, or paid work, almost always for women and girls. We drill and rehabilitate boreholes with community-elected water committees who manage them long after we've left.

  • ✅ Community-managed boreholes & rainwater harvesting systems
  • ✅ Hygiene and sanitation education in schools and households
  • ✅ Water committee training on maintenance and small repairs
  • ✅ Household latrine construction support
62 water points built · 40,000+ people with improved access
Program 03

Health & Nutrition

The nearest clinic can be a day's walk away. Our mobile health teams and trained community health workers bring maternal care, child immunization, and malnutrition screening directly into villages — and refer critical cases onward.

  • ✅ Monthly mobile clinics covering 45 villages
  • ✅ Maternal & newborn health support, including birth kits
  • ✅ Malnutrition screening and supplementary feeding referrals
  • ✅ Training for 120+ volunteer community health workers
58,000+ patient visits · 120 trained community health workers
Program 04

Women's Economic Empowerment

We organize women into savings-and-loan groups, then layer on vocational training and market access support — tailoring, agro-processing, small trade — so members build income that's entirely their own.

  • ✅ Village savings & loan associations (VSLAs)
  • ✅ Vocational training in tailoring, hairdressing & agro-processing
  • ✅ Micro-loans for first-time small business owners
  • ✅ Market linkage support connecting groups to buyers
6,200+ women trained · 300+ active savings groups
Program 05

Sustainable Agriculture & Food Security

Erratic rainfall and depleted soil put food security at constant risk. We train farming households in climate-smart techniques and maintain community seed banks so a bad season doesn't mean losing next year's crop too.

  • ✅ Climate-smart farming and soil health training
  • ✅ Community seed banks for drought-resistant varieties
  • ✅ Farming cooperative formation and market access
  • ✅ Small-scale irrigation and drip-garden pilots
4,800 farming households · 22 active cooperatives
Program 06

Community Resilience & Emergency Relief

Floods and droughts are increasingly frequent across the region. We maintain a rapid-response fund and community-managed resilience reserves so villages can respond within days, not months — and rebuild faster afterward.

  • ✅ Emergency food, shelter & hygiene kit distribution
  • ✅ Community-managed resilience funds for local response
  • ✅ Early-warning coordination with local government
  • ✅ Post-disaster livelihood recovery grants
12 emergency responses since 2021 · 8,500 households reached
How we work

Our approach in four steps

1. Listen

Village leadership and residents identify their own priorities through open community meetings.

2. Co-design

We build a program plan jointly with local committees, including who owns what long-term.

3. Deliver

Programs launch with local staff and volunteers leading day-to-day implementation.

4. Measure & hand off

We track outcomes publicly and transition full ownership to the community as it's ready.
