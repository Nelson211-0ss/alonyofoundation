Our Programs

Six core goals guide our charitable purpose, as filed in our Articles of Incorporation — each one designed to reinforce the others as we build toward our first programs in Omeo and other underserved communities in South Sudan.

Clean Water & WASH Humanitarian Assistance Health & Well-Being Education Economic Resilience Partnerships
Core Goal 01

Clean Water & WASH

Nationally, an estimated 59% of people in South Sudan lack access to safe water, and only 10% have access to improved sanitation (UNICEF). Our core goal: expand access to safe water and strengthen sanitation and hygiene systems that reduce preventable illness. Per our Articles of Incorporation, our charitable purposes include improving access to safe and clean water in underserved communities, and supporting water, sanitation, and hygiene programs and infrastructure.

  • ✅ Community water point construction and rehabilitation
  • ✅ Hygiene and sanitation education for households and schools
  • ✅ Water committee training for long-term upkeep
  • ✅ WASH needs assessments to prioritize the highest-need areas
Core Goal 02

Humanitarian & Essential Assistance

Our core goal: provide timely food, household, hygiene, sanitary, and personal-care support to people facing poverty, displacement, disaster, or hardship. Per our Articles of Incorporation, this means supporting vulnerable children, women, elderly persons, families, and other individuals experiencing poverty or humanitarian hardship.

  • ✅ Essential household and hygiene supply distribution
  • ✅ Support prioritized for vulnerable children, women, elders & families
  • ✅ Emergency and disaster relief response
  • ✅ Needs assessments to guide where help goes first
Core Goal 03

Health & Well-Being

Our core goal: support community health, nutrition, disease prevention, maternal and child health, and wellness initiatives.

  • ✅ Community health and disease-prevention initiatives
  • ✅ Maternal and child health support
  • ✅ Nutrition and wellness programs
  • ✅ Partnerships with local health providers
Core Goal 04

Education & Opportunity

An estimated 2.8 million children in South Sudan are out of school (UNICEF). Our core goal: increase access to education, learning resources, skills development, and pathways that improve long-term opportunity.

  • ✅ Access to education and learning resources
  • ✅ Skills development and training
  • ✅ Support for underserved students and communities
  • ✅ Partnerships with local schools
Core Goal 05

Economic & Community Resilience

Our core goal: advance poverty reduction, livelihoods, food security, and sustainable community-development initiatives.

  • ✅ Livelihood and income-generating support
  • ✅ Food security and sustainable agriculture initiatives
  • ✅ Poverty-reduction programs
  • ✅ Community-development planning led by residents
Core Goal 06

Partnership & Resource Mobilization

Our core goal: build accountable partnerships and secure donations, grants, gifts, and institutional support to expand charitable impact. Per our Articles of Incorporation, this includes working with nonprofit organizations, governmental institutions, community organizations, charitable foundations, international organizations, and other entities in furtherance of our charitable purposes.

  • ✅ Partnerships with nonprofit, government & community organizations
  • ✅ Transparent grant management and donor reporting
  • ✅ A diversified fundraising strategy — gifts, grants, sponsorships
  • ✅ Financial controls and procurement standards from day one
How we work

Our approach in four steps

1. Listen

Village leadership and residents identify their own priorities through open community meetings.

2. Co-design

We build a program plan jointly with local committees, including who owns what long-term.

3. Deliver

Programs launch with local staff and volunteers leading day-to-day implementation.

4. Measure & hand off

We track outcomes publicly and transition full ownership to the community as it's ready.
