Who we are

A foundation rooted in community.

Alonyo Foundation is a community-led organization partnering with families across Magwi County, South Sudan — bringing clean water, education, healthcare, and economic opportunity to the villages we call home.

Today we're a community-led foundation working across education, water, health, and economic empowerment — always led by the people closest to the problem.

What we do

Six ways we plan to build lasting change

Our charitable purpose, as filed in our Articles of Incorporation, spans six core goals — each designed with, not for, the communities we serve.

Clean Water & WASH

Expand access to safe water and strengthen sanitation and hygiene systems that reduce preventable illness.

Learn more

Humanitarian & Essential Assistance

Provide timely food, household, hygiene, sanitary, and personal-care support to people facing poverty, displacement, disaster, or hardship.

Learn more

Health & Well-Being

Support community health, nutrition, disease prevention, maternal and child health, and wellness initiatives.

Learn more

Education & Opportunity

Increase access to education, learning resources, skills development, and pathways that improve long-term opportunity.

Learn more

Economic & Community Resilience

Advance poverty reduction, livelihoods, food security, and sustainable community-development initiatives.

Partnership & Resource Mobilization

Build accountable partnerships and secure donations, grants, gifts, and institutional support to expand charitable impact.

Why Omeo

We're starting where the need — and the connection — is real.

Omeo and the surrounding communities of Magwi County, South Sudan face exactly the challenges our charitable purpose exists to address: unreliable access to clean water, thin health infrastructure, and limited educational opportunity. It's also a community our founding board has direct ties to — which is why it's where we're choosing to start, rather than where it would simply be easiest.
