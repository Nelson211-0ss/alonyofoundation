Who we are

A foundation rooted in community.

Alonyo Foundation is a community-led organization partnering with families across Magwi County, South Sudan — bringing clean water, education, healthcare, and economic opportunity to the villages we call home.

Today we're a community-led foundation working across education, water, health, and economic empowerment — always led by the people closest to the problem.

From the field

"The borehole meant my daughters could finally stay in school."

Before the well arrived in Omeo, Sunday's daughters walked three hours a day to fetch water from the river — time that came straight out of the school day. Six months after Alonyo Foundation and the village water committee installed a new borehole, both girls are back in class full-time, and the surrounding farms report higher yields from a nearby drip garden fed by the same source.
