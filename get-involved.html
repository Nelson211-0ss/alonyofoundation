Questions

Donor FAQ

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Yes, for donors in jurisdictions where Alonyo Foundation holds recognized nonprofit status. A receipt is emailed automatically after every gift — reach out if you need it reissued.

How much of my donation goes directly to programs?

100% of individual donations go directly to program delivery. Core operating costs are covered through separate multi-year grants from institutional partners — see our Annual Report for the full breakdown.

Can I designate my gift to a specific program?

Yes — select a program area at checkout, or email donate@alonyofoundation.org to discuss a restricted gift for a specific village or initiative.

How do I cancel or change a monthly gift?

You can manage or cancel recurring gifts anytime from the link in your donation confirmation email, or by contacting our team directly.

Do you accept in-kind or corporate matching gifts?

Yes — many employers match employee donations dollar-for-dollar. Check with your HR department, and let us know so we can help process the match.
