Alonyo Foundation Incorporates as an Iowa Nonprofit

We've filed our Articles of Incorporation under Chapter 504 of the Iowa Code, organized exclusively for charitable and educational purposes, and are pursuing recognition as a 501(c)(3) tax-exempt organization.
